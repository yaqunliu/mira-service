# 分镜技巧知识库

## 建立镜头 (Establishing Shot)

**用途**：介绍场景、时间、地点，让观众了解故事发生的环境

**构图特点**：
- 超广角，展示环境全貌
- 通常无角色或角色很小
- 强调空间感和氛围

**时长建议**：3-5秒

**提示词模板**：
```
Establishing shot of [location], [time of day], [weather], wide angle, cinematic, [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Establishing shot of a traditional Chinese village, early morning, misty mountains in background, wide angle, cinematic lighting, anime style
```

---

## 中景镜头 (Medium Shot)

**用途**：对话场景、角色互动，展示角色上半身

**构图特点**：
- 角色腰部以上入画
- 可容纳2-3人
- 保持适当头部空间

**时长建议**：2-4秒

**提示词模板**：
```
Medium shot of [character] [action], [expression], [background], [lighting], [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Medium shot of two friends talking in a cafe, warm expressions, bokeh background, soft natural lighting, realistic style
```

---

## 特写镜头 (Close-up Shot)

**用途**：展示情感、重要细节，强调角色内心

**构图特点**：
- 面部或物体特写
- 背景模糊
- 聚焦于眼睛或关键表情

**时长建议**：1-3秒

**提示词模板**：
```
Close-up of [subject], [emotion/detail], [lighting], shallow depth of field, [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Close-up of a young woman's face, tears in her eyes, dramatic side lighting, shallow depth of field, cinematic
```

---

## 动作镜头 (Action Shot)

**用途**：打斗、追逐、动态场景，展示运动和冲击力

**构图特点**：
- 动态角度
- 运动模糊
- 冲击线/速度线
- 夸张透视

**时长建议**：1-2秒

**提示词模板**：
```
Dynamic action shot of [character] [action], motion blur, [angle], dramatic composition, [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Dynamic action shot of a warrior leaping through the air, sword raised, motion blur, low angle, dramatic lighting, anime style
```

---

## 过渡镜头 (Transition Shot)

**用途**：场景转换、时间流逝、情绪过渡

**构图特点**：
- 象征性元素
- 自然景观
- 抽象画面

**时长建议**：2-3秒

**提示词模板**：
```
[Symbolic element] transitioning from [state A] to [state B], [atmosphere], [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Cherry blossoms falling from a tree, transitioning from spring to summer, peaceful atmosphere, watercolor style
```

---

## 反应镜头 (Reaction Shot)

**用途**：展示角色对事件的反应，增强情感共鸣

**构图特点**：
- 聚焦于反应者的表情
- 可用特写或中景
- 注意时机和节奏

**提示词模板**：
```
Reaction shot of [character], [emotional response], [lighting], [style]
```

---

## 主观镜头 (POV Shot)

**用途**：第一人称视角，让观众代入角色

**构图特点**：
- 模拟角色视线
- 可能有手部入画
- 增加代入感

**提示词模板**：
```
POV shot, [what character sees], first person perspective, [style]
```

## 视频运动指南

### 镜头运动类型

| 运动类型 | 英文 | 描述 |
|---------|------|------|
| 平移 | camera pan left/right | 镜头左右水平移动 |
| 推拉 | camera zoom in/out | 镜头推进或拉远 |
| 倾斜 | camera tilt up/down | 镜头上下倾斜 |
| 推进 | dolly forward/backward | 摄像机前进后退 |
| 环绕 | orbit around subject | 环绕主体拍摄 |
| 升降 | crane shot | 垂直升降运动 |
| 跟踪 | tracking shot | 跟随主体移动 |

### 运动描述关键词

- 慢动作：slow motion, slowmo
- 快动作：fast motion, speed up
- 平滑运动：smooth camera movement
- 动态抖动：dynamic camera shake, handheld feel
- 静止镜头：static shot, locked camera, tripod shot

### 视频提示词示例

```
Camera slowly zooms in on character's face, subtle expression change, soft lighting remains constant
```

```
Smooth tracking shot following character walking through crowded market, ambient lighting, bustling atmosphere
```

```
Dynamic camera shake during explosion, debris flying, dramatic slow motion, cinematic action
```
