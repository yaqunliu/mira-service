# 角色图片提示词模板

## 基础结构

```
[角色身份] + [外貌特征] + [服装描述] + [姿态表情] + [光线环境] + [风格关键词]
```

---

## 角色类型模板

### 主角 (Protagonist)
```
[Name], a [age] year old [gender] [occupation/role], [distinctive features], wearing [clothing description], [pose/action], [expression], [lighting], [background], [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Young hero, a 18 year old male warrior, with spiky black hair and determined eyes, wearing battle-worn armor with a red cape, standing heroically with sword raised, confident expression, dramatic backlight, ancient battlefield background, anime style, dynamic pose
```

### 反派 (Antagonist)
```
[Villain type], [age/appearance], [menacing features], wearing [dark/imposing clothing], [threatening pose], [sinister expression], [dramatic lighting], [ominous background], [style]
```

**示例**：
```
Dark sorcerer, ageless appearance with pale skin and glowing red eyes, wearing flowing black robes with silver runes, casting dark magic with raised hands, cruel smile, underlighting with purple glow, throne room with shadows, fantasy art style
```

### 配角 (Supporting Character)
```
[Role relationship], [age] [gender], [friendly/neutral features], wearing [practical clothing], [casual pose], [appropriate expression], [natural lighting], [relevant background], [style]
```

## 外貌描述词库

### 发型
- 长发：long flowing hair, waist-length hair, silky straight hair
- 短发：short cropped hair, pixie cut, messy short hair
- 特殊：twin tails, braided hair, ponytail, bun hairstyle
- 颜色：jet black, platinum blonde, fiery red, silver-white

### 眼睛
- 形状：large expressive eyes, sharp narrow eyes, gentle round eyes
- 颜色：deep blue eyes, emerald green eyes, golden amber eyes
- 特点：heterochromia, glowing eyes, tearful eyes

### 表情
- 积极：warm smile, confident grin, gentle expression
- 消极：sorrowful look, angry scowl, fearful expression
- 中性：stoic expression, mysterious smile, thoughtful gaze

---

## 服装描述词库

### 现代服装
- 休闲：casual outfit, jeans and t-shirt, comfortable streetwear
- 正式：business suit, formal dress, elegant attire
- 校服：school uniform, sailor uniform, blazer and tie

### 古风服装
- 中式：traditional hanfu, flowing robes, silk garments
- 日式：kimono, hakama, traditional yukata
- 西式：medieval armor, Victorian dress, Renaissance attire

### 奇幻服装
- 战士：battle armor, leather gear, warrior outfit
- 法师：wizard robes, mystical cloak, enchanted garments
- 贵族：royal attire, noble dress, jeweled accessories
